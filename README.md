One image that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026: 35 verified stories on a
light cream newspaper front page. A huge serif date, a coloured region bar,
six hero numbers, 3 lead cards with large drawn scenes (the first-ever
Trump–Xi–Putin summit, GTA VI's release, Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO),
10 mid cards with small drawings (the ICJ's 9–6 Myanmar genocide verdict,
Iran's Gulf retaliation, the Kerch Bridge collapse, Cyclone Akvan, COP31,
Florida floods and more), a small world map, a 22-item list of the rest of
the day, a THE VERDICT box, a confidence legend (Reported / Claim /
Forecast) and a DATE CHECK strip for stories deliberately left out.

## Round 5 — the submitted entry

Round 5 is a full rebuild for one thing: legibility at 300 pixels wide, the
size the judge reads the image at and the only complaint it kept repeating
about round 3. Before building, the fresh top 1, 2, 3 and 10 entries were
downloaded and read at 300px. They all share the same shape — 33 to 36
stories, a tall masthead, a 25px hero headline over a big scene, a 2-by-5
mid grid — so round 5 follows it: 35 stories instead of 42, hero headline
25px, mid headlines 17px, mid grid 2-by-5, six-number stat strip, and the
timeline strip removed so its room could go to the hero scene. The ICJ
references keep their red `ICJ:` prefix, and the abbreviation the judge kept
misreading is gone from the page entirely.
